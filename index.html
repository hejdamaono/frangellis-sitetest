A South Philly tradition

Some things
shouldn't change.

Frangelli's Bakery opened in 1947 when Joseph Frangelli started the bakery at 9th & Jackson in South Philadelphia.

In 1994, the bakery moved a few blocks south to 9th & Ritner, where it continues to serve the neighborhood today.

The recipes, traditions, and hands-on approach are what make Frangelli's feel like a true neighborhood bakery.

Our story
1947

A bakery built
around tradition.

Joseph Frangelli opened Frangelli's Bakery in South Philadelphia in 1947 at 9th & Jackson.

Nearly five decades later, the bakery moved to its current home at 9th & Ritner in 1994.

Today, Frangelli's continues the tradition with doughnuts, cakes, cookies, pastries and the neighborhood specialties that have made the bakery a South Philly staple.
