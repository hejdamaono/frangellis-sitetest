A South Philly tradition

Some things
shouldn't change.

Frangelli's Bakery opened in 1947 when Joseph Frangelli started the bakery at 9th & Jackson in South Philadelphia.

In 1994, the bakery moved a few blocks south to 9th & Ritner, where it continues to serve the neighborhood today.

The recipes, traditions, and hands-on approach are what make Frangelli's feel like a true neighborhood bakery.

Born in South Philly

Meet the
Franolli.

The Franolli originated in 2015 when ricotta was being made for cannoli. A piece of doughnut was broken off, topped with the ricotta, and the combination turned out to be delicious.

Mini Franollis were made for customers to try, along with larger ones to sell. One customer who tried one knew someone at Zagat and told them about it. A few weeks later, Zagat wrote about the Franolli, and it took off from there.

The Franolli has since been featured on FYI Philly, The Chew, The Steve Harvey Show, and The Harry Connick Jr. Show.

Philly loves the Franolli because it is truly Philly. It was born and raised in South Philly. Every Franolli is hand cut one at a time, filled individually with love.

NY has the Cronut. Philly has the Franolli.

Stop in and try the original Franolli, a chocolate Franolli, or the seasonal favorite — pumpkin Franolli.
