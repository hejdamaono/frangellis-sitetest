EST. 1947

Philadelphia, PA

Joseph Frangelli opened Frangelli's Bakery in 1947 at 9th & Jackson Streets. The bakery eventually moved to its current location on West Ritner Street in 1994.

Today, the bakery continues the tradition of handmade doughnuts, cakes, cookies and other Italian-American favorites.

The focus has stayed simple: make great food by hand, serve the neighborhood, and give people a reason to come back.

PHILLY'S OWN

Meet the
Franolli.

The Franolli was born at Frangelli's in 2015 while experimenting with cannoli cream and doughnut dough.

While making the ricotta filling for cannoli, a piece of doughnut was combined with the cream. The combination turned out to be surprisingly good.

“NY has the Cronut.
Philly has the Franolli.”

What started as an experiment quickly became one of Frangelli's signature creations and a South Philly favorite.

The Franolli has been featured on FYI Philly, The Chew, The Steve Harvey Show and The Harry Connick Jr. Show.
