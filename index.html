OUR STORY

SOUTH PHILADELPHIA · 1947

A bakery with
a long memory.

Joseph Frangelli opened his bakery on the corner of 9th & Jackson Streets in South Philadelphia in 1947.

In 1994, the bakery moved a few blocks south to 9th & Ritner Streets, where Frangelli's remains today.

Through generations, the bakery has continued making traditional pastries while creating new favorites for the neighborhood.
