A South Philly tradition

Some things are better when they're made the old-fashioned way.

Frangelli's Bakery has been part of South Philadelphia since 1947. What started as a neighborhood bakery grew into a place people travel across the city for.

The bakery is known for doing things by hand — from cutting doughnuts to pumping each one to order. The result is simple, fresh, and unmistakably Frangelli's.

The Philly original

Meet the Franolli.

New York has the Cronut. Philly has the Franolli.

The Franolli came from an accidental combination of doughnut and cannoli that turned into one of Frangelli's most recognizable creations.

What began as a little experiment eventually became a South Philly favorite and earned attention far beyond the neighborhood.
